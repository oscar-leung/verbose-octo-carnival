Serifu 台詞 — Privacy Policy

Effective: July 2026

Serifu is a language-practice app where friends watch media together and practice speaking. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

No accounts, no profiles

There is no sign-up. You choose a display name per session; it is held in memory on the server only while your practice room exists and is deleted when the room empties.

Your media never leaves your device

Video and audio files you open stay on your device. Only playback timing (play/pause/position) and the practice script are shared with the people in your room.

Microphone

With your permission, the microphone is used for two features: voice chat (sent peer-to-peer to the people in your room over encrypted WebRTC) and speech scoring (processed by your browser's built-in speech recognition — in Chrome/Edge this may involve your browser vendor's speech service, per that browser's own privacy policy). Serifu's server never receives or stores your audio.

Stored on your device only

Display settings, your wordbook, mastery progress, episode tracker, and saved scripts live in your browser's local storage. Clearing site data removes them. None of it is uploaded.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Serifu contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and sets no tracking cookies.

Data we technically process

While a room is active, the server relays: display names, chosen characters, playback timing, the loaded script, spoken-attempt scores, and voice-chat connection signals. All of it is in-memory only and is discarded when the room closes. Standard web-server logs (IP addresses, request paths) are handled by our hosting provider (Render) per their policy.

Children

Serifu is a general-audience study tool and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
