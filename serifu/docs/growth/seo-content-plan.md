# Serifu — SEO Content Plan (long-tail, compounding)

Strategy: own the "speak Japanese using shows you love" cluster before anyone else does.
Every article is genuinely useful standalone (works even without Serifu), mentions
Serifu once as the tool that automates the workflow, and stays anime-agnostic (genres
and formats, never show names). Site structure: marketing site with `/blog/` for
articles and `/guides/` for evergreen how-tos; the app itself links back via footer.

## Articles

1. **How to Practice Speaking Japanese with Anime (Without a Tutor)**
   The cluster head: why output beats passive immersion, the pause-speak-compare loop,
   and how to turn any episode into a speaking drill. — Lives at `/guides/speak-japanese-with-anime`
2. **Japanese Shadowing with Friends: Why Group Shadowing Beats Solo**
   The accountability/fear argument with a concrete session format for 2–4 people.
   Targets "shadowing japanese with friends", "group shadowing". — `/blog/shadowing-with-friends`
3. **What Is Anime Shadowing? A Beginner's Step-by-Step Method**
   Definition-style post for the "what is" query; ladder from listen-repeat to
   voice-the-character. — `/blog/what-is-anime-shadowing`
4. **How to Get Japanese Subtitles for Any Show (.srt, .vtt, .ass Explained)**
   Practical sourcing + format guide (jimaku/kitsunekko class archives, legality notes,
   speaker tags in .ass). Huge search volume, perfect Serifu-importer tie-in. — `/guides/japanese-subtitle-files`
5. **Furigana Practice: Reading Along with Native-Speed Japanese**
   How furigana scripts bridge the kanji gap while listening; when to wean off.
   Targets "furigana reader", "japanese subtitles with furigana". — `/blog/furigana-practice`
6. **Japanese Watch Party: How to Watch and Study an Episode Together Online**
   Setup guide for synced study sessions (sync options, voice chat, session structure).
   Targets "japanese watch party", "study japanese with friends online". — `/guides/japanese-watch-party`
7. **Too Scared to Speak Japanese? A Gentler Path Than Talking to Strangers**
   Empathy-led piece on language anxiety; scripted lines with friends as exposure
   therapy. Targets "afraid to speak japanese", "japanese speaking anxiety". — `/blog/scared-to-speak-japanese`
8. **Spaced Repetition for Sentences: Why Drilling Lines Beats Drilling Words**
   Sentence-level SRS, memory-practice pages, and how spoken recall strengthens
   retention. Targets "sentence srs japanese", "spaced repetition sentences". — `/blog/sentence-srs`
9. **Language Reactor vs Migaku vs Serifu: Which Immersion Tool for Speaking?**
   Honest comparison — those tools win at reading/mining input, Serifu at spoken output
   with friends. Targets high-intent comparison queries. — `/blog/immersion-tools-compared`
10. **Can Speech Recognition Grade Your Japanese Pronunciation? What It Can and Can't Tell You**
    Honest explainer of edit-distance scoring vs true pitch/pronunciation feedback.
    Targets "japanese pronunciation checker app". — `/blog/speech-recognition-japanese`

## Internal linking notes

- **Hub-and-spoke:** #1 is the pillar. Every other article links up to #1 in its intro
  or conclusion; #1 links out to all nine as "next steps". This concentrates authority
  on the head term "practice speaking japanese with anime".
- **Natural pairs:** #2 ↔ #3 (methods), #4 ↔ #5 (subtitles/reading), #6 ↔ #2 (group
  sessions), #7 ↔ #2 (anxiety → friends), #8 ↔ #10 (scoring/retention mechanics).
- **Money paths:** #4, #6, and #9 are the highest-intent pages — each ends with a
  single, clearly marked CTA into the app's create-a-room page. Others link to the
  guides, not the app, to keep them evergreen and shareable.
- **App → content:** the in-app subtitle importer links to #4; the empty episode
  library links to #6; the solo practice page links to #8. These deep links get the
  guides indexed fast and make onboarding self-serve.
- Publish #1, #4, #7 first (pillar + highest volume + most shareable), then one per
  week. Update #9 quarterly; comparison pages decay fastest.
