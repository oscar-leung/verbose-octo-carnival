## Full description (~2500 chars)

You can pass every vocab quiz and still freeze the moment you have to speak.
Speaking practice is the part of Japanese that's scary, lonely, and easiest to skip —
so most learners never do it.

Serifu fixes that with something you already love: your shows. Pick an episode you and
your friends own, join a room, and the app keeps everyone's playback perfectly in sync.
A timed script scrolls beside the video with furigana readings and English translations.
Each of you claims a character — and when your character's line comes up, the video
pauses. You deliver the line before you ever hear the real actor.

Here's the magic: in rehearsal mode, Serifu listens. Say your line and it's scored live
against the script (kanji or kana reading both count). Hit 70 or above and the video
auto-resumes — no clicking, just the rush of nailing a line and the story rolling on.
Miss it? See what the app heard, laugh about it with your friends over the built-in
voice chat, and try again. Everyone in the room watches your attempts land in real time.
Terrifying alone, hilarious together.

WHAT YOU GET
• Synced watch-party playback — anyone can pause, seek, replay a line
• Rehearsal mode: auto-pause on your lines, live speech scoring, auto-resume on a pass
• Furigana scripts with English translations, generated from your own subtitle files
• Script editor: import .srt / .vtt / .ass subtitles, tag speakers, shift timings
• Episode library: prep an episode once, reload it any week with one click
• Built-in voice chat for talking through phrases between lines
• Solo practice pages for drilling vocab and lines between sessions, with spaced
  repetition so what you learned actually sticks

YOUR MEDIA, YOUR PRIVACY
Serifu is a practice tool, not a streaming service. It ships with a short original demo
scene only — you bring your own legally obtained episodes and subtitles. Video files
never leave your device; only timing signals and your script are shared with your room.
No account required.

HONEST NOTES
• Live speech scoring uses your device's speech recognition (best in Chrome-based
  browsers/Android). Where unavailable, a manual continue button works everywhere.
• Built for small groups (2–6 friends), not giant rooms.

Stop studying Japanese silently. Grab two friends, pick an episode, and say the lines.
